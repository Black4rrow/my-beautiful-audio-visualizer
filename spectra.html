Capturing your PC audio

Your browser will open a sharing window. Here's what to do in it:

Chrome tabWindowEntire screen
Also share system audio
CancelShare
  1. Open the Entire screen tab.
  2. Click on your screen to select it.
  3. Turn on Also share system audio (« Partager aussi l'audio du système » if your browser is in French).
  4. Click Share.

Only the sound is analysed: the screen image is never used or recorded. To stop, click “Stop sharing” in the bar Chrome shows at the bottom.
